Feed Water Type and System Recovery Estimation
This document outlines the guidelines and standard baselines for estimating membrane system recovery based on feed water sources. It provides foundational logic for feed water input, validation modules, and membrane performance projection.
Primary Limiting Factors
Water type acts as a proxy for two main system constraints:
Osmotic Pressure Limit: 
Higher salt concentrations (e.g., seawater) require more pressure. As recovery increases, rejected salts concentrate in the feed stream, raising osmotic pressure until hydraulic limits are reached.
Solubility Limits (Scaling): 
For lower salinity waters (e.g., well or brackish water), sparingly soluble salts concentrate in the reject stream as clean water is recovered. High recovery can cause these salts to exceed saturation points and scale the membrane.
